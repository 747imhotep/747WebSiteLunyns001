## 🌐 HIGH-LEVEL STRUCTURE

**Home Page → Role Selection → Verification & Declarations → Redirect (Due Diligence / NCNDA)**

---

## 🧭 FLOW OVERVIEW (Step-by-Step)

### **1️⃣ Landing Page**

**Goal:** Welcome visitors, clearly explain your platform’s purpose and roles.

**Elements:**

* Brief tagline:
  *“Connecting serious buyers and sellers in global commodities.”*
* Button: **“Start Verification”** → leads to role selection

---

### **2️⃣ Role Selection Page**

**Question:** *“Who are you?”*

**6 options:**

1. End Seller
2. End Buyer
3. Mandate Seller
4. Mandate Buyer
5. Broker (Seller Side)
6. Broker (Buyer Side)

**UX Tips:**

* Use large clickable cards with icons
* Hover tooltips that explain each role briefly

🡒 After selection → moves to **Confirmation Step**

---

### **3️⃣ Confirmation & Information Form**

**Dynamic form changes depending on role selected.**

**Questions:**

1. Confirm your role (auto-filled from previous page)
2. Confirm you understand we act as brokers/intermediaries
3. Do you have these documents? (multi-select checkboxes)

   * SCO
   * LOI
   * ICPO
   * Draft Contract
4. Do you have a target price? (Y/N + input box if yes)
5. Do you own a registered company? (Y/N)
6. Do you have a mandate letter? (Y/N)

✅ **Form auto-validates answers** (shows next steps in real time)

---

### **4️⃣ Logic-Based Routing**

| User Type              | Has Company | Has Mandate Letter | Redirects To              |
| ---------------------- | ----------- | ------------------ | ------------------------- |
| End Buyer / End Seller | (any)       | (any)              | **Due Diligence**         |
| Mandate Buyer / Seller | Yes         | Yes                | **Due Diligence → NCNDA** |
| Mandate Buyer / Seller | No          | No                 | **NCNDA only**            |
| Broker (any side)      | Yes         | –                  | **Due Diligence → NCNDA** |
| Broker (any side)      | No          | –                  | **NCNDA only**            |

🡒 Once routed, user gets a “Next Steps” summary screen.

---

### **5️⃣ Redirect Pages**

#### **Due Diligence Page**

**Purpose:** Gather professional information to verify legitimacy.

Fields include:

* Company info (name, registration number, address)
* Contact details
* Product(s) of interest (petroleum, coconuts, etc.)
* Region(s) of operation
* Upload supporting documents (e.g. registration cert.)

🡒 After submission → automatic transition to **NCNDA** (if required)

---

#### **NCNDA Page**

**Purpose:** Non-Circumvention, Non-Disclosure Agreement

* Auto-filled with user name & company details
* e-Signature capability (DocuSign or custom signature field)
* Downloadable PDF after signing
* Confirmation email sent

---

### **6️⃣ Final Confirmation Page**

**Message example:**

> ✅ Thank you, \[Name]!
> Your submission has been received. Our compliance team will review your documents and contact you within 48 hours.

Include:

* Reference number
* Contact button for support
* Option to explore product listings while waiting

---

## 💡 DESIGN TIPS

* **Progress bar:**
  *“Step 1 of 4: Role → Confirmation → Verification → Done”*
* **Conditional logic:** Form dynamically shows only relevant fields.
  (Example: Brokers skip mandate questions.)
* **Trust elements:**

  * Secure badges (SSL, data protection)
  * Professional tone
  * Transparent contact info
* **Language:**
  Use plain terms for global users: “Do you have your own company?” instead of “Are you incorporated?”
