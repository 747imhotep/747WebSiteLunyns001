🧪 CNSL

Cashew Nuts Shell Liquid (CNSL) is a versatile natural product derived from the shells of cashew nuts. It is widely used in various industrial applications due to its unique chemical properties.

🧪 CNSL

Cashew Nuts Shell Liquid

CNSL is rich in phenolic compounds, mainly:

  • Anacardic acid (60–70%)
  • Cardanol (15–20%)
  • Cardol and 2-methylcardol (10–15%)


    • Degummed Cashew Nut Shell Liquid (CNSL)

    We supply Degummed Cashew Nut Shell Liquid (CNSL) directly from a Vietnam-based manufacturer with a monthly production capacity of 5,000 MT (Metric Tons).

    This bio-based industrial oil is derived from cashew nut shells, offering a renewable alternative to petroleum-based phenolic compounds used in the resins, coatings, friction, and polymer industries.

    Our degumming process removes gums, phospholipids, and polar impurities from the crude CNSL, resulting in a high-purity, stable, and consistent feedstock suitable for technical and chemical applications.

    Key Specifications:

    The product is ISCC-EU certified, ensuring full compliance with international sustainability and traceability standards.
    Smaller trial batches are available for formulation development, pilot testing, and quality assessment.

    CNSL is a valuable bio-based chemical feedstock used in:

    1. Resins & Coatings
  • Used to make friction materials, paints, and varnishes.
  • Improves heat resistance and durability.
    • 2. Brake Linings & Clutch Facings
  • Cardanol-based resins are used in brake pads — resistant to high temperature and wear.
    • 3. Rubber & Plastic Additives
  • Used as a plasticizer or modifier to improve flexibility and strength.
    • 4. Surface-active Agents (Surfactants)
  • For detergents, emulsifiers, and anti-corrosive agents.
    • 5. Biofuel & Lubricant Precursors
  • Can be refined into renewable hydrocarbons or used as a base for bio-lubricants.
    • 6. Pharmaceutical & Agricultural Chemicals (in small amounts)
  • Some derivatives have antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-termite properties.
